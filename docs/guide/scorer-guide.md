# Scorer guide

One page. Read it before the round starts.

**Words used here**

- **Chamber**: a team. Each chamber has a card on your screen.
- **Decibels (dB)**: the points. A chamber's total can go below 0.
- **Round**: the admin switches the round (Adagio, Andante, Allegro, Vivace, Awarding). Your screen changes by itself.
- **Undo**: cancels one entry. The entry stays in the history, crossed out.

## 1. Open the scoreboard

1. Connect the laptop to the event Wi-Fi hotspot. The admin gives you the network name and password.
2. Open the browser and go to the address the admin gives you. It looks like `http://192.168.137.10:3000`. Use the bookmark if one was set up.
3. The screen shows the current round name at the top, the chamber cards on the left and the scoring panel on the right.
4. If it says **Connecting…** for more than a few seconds, tell the admin.

## 2. Add or subtract points: two taps

1. **Tap the chamber card.** The card gets a gold border and the chamber's name appears above the buttons. Until you pick a chamber the panel says **Tap a chamber first** and the buttons are greyed out.
2. **Tap the points button.** Green buttons with a solid edge add points, for example **+20**. Red buttons with a dashed edge subtract, for example **−30**.
3. A message appears at the bottom: **+20 to Chamber 1**, with an **Undo** button. It stays for 6 seconds.
4. The chamber's total changes at once, and the entry appears under the card with its reason, for example **+20 Correct**.

A small gold, silver or bronze number appears on the cards of the chambers in 1st, 2nd and 3rd place. It is only for information. A place that is still tied has no medal.

The chamber stays selected, so for the next entry to the same chamber you only tap the points button. For a different chamber, tap its card first.

Easy, Medium and Hard are always the first, second and third button.

## 4. Any other amount: the Custom pad

Use it for a Fortissimo wager, a Crescendo bonus, or any correction that has no button.

1. Tap the chamber card.
2. Under **Custom**, tap **+** to add or **−** to subtract. The chosen sign is filled in.
3. Tap the digits. The amount shows above the keys. Tap **⌫** to delete the last digit. The largest amount is 1000.
4. Tap **Add +45** (the button shows the amount you typed). Nothing happens while the amount is 0.

## 5. Undo a mistake

Three ways, all the same result:

- **Right after the tap:** tap **Undo** on the message at the bottom (within 6 seconds).
- **A little later:** every card lists its last 3 entries, each with a small **Undo** button. Tap the one that is wrong.
- **Any other entry:** ask the admin. The admin can undo any entry from the full list.

Undo does not delete anything. It adds a cancelling entry, so the history still shows what happened. You cannot undo an undo. If you undid the wrong thing, enter the points again.

If two scorers both entered the same answer for a chamber, both show under the card. Undo one of them.

## 6. The red bar: offline

If the Wi-Fi drops, a red bar appears under the round name:

> **Offline. Your entries are saved and will send when the connection returns. (2 waiting)**

What to do:

1. **Keep scoring.** Your taps are saved on this laptop, even if you reload the page. Entries that have not been sent yet are shown in italics with **(not sent yet)** under the card.
2. Check the laptop is still connected to the event hotspot. Reconnect if it is not.
3. When the connection returns, the bar turns yellow and says **Sending… 2**, counts down, and disappears. Every saved entry is sent once. Nothing is lost and nothing is counted twice.
4. If the red bar stays for more than a minute, tell the admin. Do not close the browser tab: the unsent entries are stored in this browser.

Do not re-enter points you already tapped while offline. They will send on their own.

## 7. Do not

- Do not open the scoreboard in two tabs on the same laptop and score in both.
- Do not use the browser's back button or refresh unless the admin asks. Refreshing is safe, but it is not needed.
- Do not touch the admin screen. Proctors check answers; the scoreboard only records points.
